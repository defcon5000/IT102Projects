Disclaimer: I'm not saying these are good films. However, each one adds something to the horror canon not seen anywhere else, be it a performance, a special effect, or a single unforgettable shot.

Prom Night II: Hello Mary Lou

The actress who plays Mary Lou in the flashback is incredible. My spouse and I quote the opening scene all the time.

Chopping Mall

I just rewatched this one; it improves on each watch. Part of that is knowing where it's going, and part of it is really rooting for the main quartet. All the background 80s mall details are great too.

Repo! The Genetic Opera

You'll either love it or hate it, but as someone who loves musicals and opera, I love how this truly follows operatic structure. They give their opera singers meaty songs to show off their chops. Cameo by Joan Jett, the girl from Spy Kids playing against type, and of course there's the standout Paris Hilton turn where she makes fun of herself in grand fashion.

Poltergeist III

I'm adding this for a singular scene. The film takes place in a huge city condo building, and the scene itself takes place in its massive parking garage. No spoilers, it's just visually incredible and I've never seen anything else like it.

Re-Animator

One of my faves of all time. Jeffrey Combs is a Scream King, but this is my favorite of his roles; he embodies Herbert West's strange thrall to perfection. "What did you want me to say? Cat dead, details later?"

Vampire's Kiss

Had to end on a classic, even though I don't think this is overrated at all. Possibly one of the most famous Bad Movies of all time. Still, if you haven't seen it, it's well worth a watch. More inexplicable than The Room, more disdainful than American Psycho (and it came out before American Psycho, which makes you wonder how much the former influenced the latter), and one of the great Nic Cage performances of all time.
